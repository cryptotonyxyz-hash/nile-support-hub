Demo prototype

Merchant support that merchants can actually track.

Today, Nile merchant support runs on email alone. There is no live chat, no ticket portal, and no way for a merchant to see where a request stands. This hub is a working demo of the missing system: merchants submit and track requests, chat for quick help, and the support team works from one inbox where every ticket is triaged against Nile's published SLA.

01

The gap today

Support is a contact form and one email address (business@nile.ng). A merchant who reports a down storefront waits with no ticket number, no status, and no deadline they can check.

02

What this hub adds

A ticket portal that issues a tracking ID on submission, a live chat for quick questions, and a track-my-request page that shows status, timeline and the SLA deadline for every request.

03

How the team keeps the SLA

Every ticket is auto-triaged by issue type and plan tier the moment it arrives. Deadlines come from Nile's published SLA, and tickets about to breach are flagged before they do.

The SLA this system enforces

First response times are Nile's published SLA. Resolution targets are proposed by this project, because Nile does not publish resolution times.

TrackWhat countsPremium first responseStandard first responseResolution target
Critical Storefront down, or checkout completely broken 1 hour 4 hours 24 hours Proposed
Standard All other enquiries 6 hours 12 hours 72 hours Proposed

Auto-triage rule: a ticket within 30 minutes of its first response deadline is flagged at risk. Past the deadline with no response, it is flagged breached and escalated to the top of the inbox.

About this demo. This is a portfolio prototype built for a Nile open internship application. The 16 seeded tickets are demo data using store names from Nile's public store samples; they are not real Nile tickets. Tickets you submit and chat messages you send during your visit are held in this page only, for this session, so you can see them flow into the inbox and tracking. First response deadlines use Nile's published SLA. Resolution targets (24 hours Critical, 72 hours Standard) are proposed by this project and labelled as such, because Nile publishes first response times only.
